## 🇨🇳 中文

端到端制作真人写实风格的半解说短剧：把灵感、主题或故事前提发展为聚焦创意，确认一句话梗概、角色、场景、单集结构、剧本、对白和旁白，建立可复用的角色与场景连续性锚点，再把剧本转成带时间码的分镜与镜头合同，生成角色卡、场景卡、关键帧与镜头视频，组装后读取成片真实时间轴并生成一条独立旁白音轨。支持第一人称或第三人称旁白推进，结合角色对白、动作表演与场景调度完成单集成片，并检查连续性、口型同步、构图、节奏与最终时长。默认生图 2K、视频每镜 5–15 整数秒；不用于动画、二次元、纯解说、纯口播或普通剪辑。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 从灵感、梗概到成片：一句话梗概、角色、场景、单集结构、剧本、对白与旁白逐项确认
- 默认分镜拓扑固定为一个生成视频片段对应一张三格时间分镜图，三格对应真实片段内镜头
- 每个多镜头片段产出片段内镜头表：编号、起止秒数、景别、机位、出场人物、转场与连续性衔接
- 角色卡 16:9 纯白底，左侧脸部特写 + 右侧正/侧/背半身三视图，按真实选角与服装写
- 场景卡 16:9 纯白底且不含任何人物，保持空间关系与重复物体一致
- 组装后读取成片真实时间轴生成一条独立旁白音轨；镜头失败只针对该镜重试一次，再失败才切换兼容模型

## 🇬🇧 English

End-to-end production for a live-action, photoreal half-narrated short drama: develop an idea, theme, or premise into a focused concept, confirm the one-line logline, characters, scenes, episode structure, script, dialogue, and narration, build reusable character and scene continuity anchors, then turn the script into a timecoded storyboard and shot contract. It generates character cards, scene cards, keyframes, and shot videos, assembles them in story order, reads the real timeline of the finished cut, and produces one separate narration track. First- or third-person narration drives the plot while character dialogue, performance, and staging carry the episode, with checks on continuity, lip sync, composition, pacing, and final duration. Image generation defaults to 2K and each video shot to 5–15 whole seconds; it is not for animation, anime, pure explainer or talking-head, or ordinary editing.

### ✨ What it does

- Idea and logline through to finished episode: logline, characters, scenes, episode structure, script, dialogue, and narration confirmed step by step
- Fixed default topology — one generated video segment maps to one three-panel time board whose panels match real in-segment shots
- Every multi-shot segment gets an in-segment shot table: number, start/end seconds, framing, camera, cast on screen, transition, and continuity hand-off
- Character cards are 16:9 on pure white: face close-up left, front/side/back half-body views right, written for real casting and wardrobe
- Scene cards are 16:9 on pure white with no people at all, keeping spatial relations and repeated objects consistent
- After assembly it reads the real timeline to produce one separate narration track; a failed shot retries once, then switches to a compatible model
